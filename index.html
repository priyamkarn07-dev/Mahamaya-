About MAHAMAYA

Rooted in simplicity, crafted with care

MAHAMAYA brings you foxnuts — the puffed seeds of the lotus, known in India as makhana. We focus on clean roasting and honest flavour, so every handful stays light, crisp and satisfying.

From everyday snacking to thoughtful gifting, our range spans nine flavours and multiple grades, letting you choose exactly the size and taste you love.
